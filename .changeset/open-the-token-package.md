Add `@blog/tokens`, the single source of the design system's colors, spacing, radii, focus and motion in the W3C token format, with light, dark and forced-colors values. `@blog/ui`, the landing and dashboard remotes and the shell's overlay now read it instead of keeping their own copies. New `ThemeToggle` and a `data-theme` color mode owned by the shell.
